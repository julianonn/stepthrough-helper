Classify this one frame. Answer with exactly one word: page, auth, or junk.

page: a fully drawn screen whose content can be read. This includes settings screens, account web pages, an app's inbox or home screen, lists of devices or sessions, and sheets or pop-up panels that contain their own settings or options.

auth: a screen or pop-up that asks the person to prove who they are: a passcode or PIN pad, a password field, a Face ID or fingerprint prompt, a verification code entry, a "check your phone" or approval request, or a sign-in page asking for an email, phone number, or password.

junk: anything else: a keyboard covering most of the screen, a loading spinner or blank screen, a frame caught mid-transition where two screens overlap or text is blurred, the device's home screen, or a small system dialog with no settings (such as "Open in app?").
